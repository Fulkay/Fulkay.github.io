  ## Why FulKay?

  FulKay brings together practical analytical projects, technical learning and writing in one place. The aim is to explain not just *what* a model or method does, but *why* it works, when it is useful and where its limitations lie.

  ## From Blognation to FulKay

  This site continues a personal development journey that began with **Blognation**, a Django website built in 2017. The original application is being preserved separately; FulKay is its lightweight, modern successor.

  ## A note on published work

  Examples and case studies shared here are independently prepared for educational purposes. Public-sector material will be published only where appropriate permissions and data safeguards are in place.
